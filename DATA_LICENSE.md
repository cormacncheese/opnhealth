# Data licence

The OpnHealth database (everything served by the public API, shown on the
website, and published in the nightly exports) is made available under the
[Open Database License (ODbL) v1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).
Individual contents are under the
[Database Contents License v1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/dbcl/1-0/).

ODbL is required because the catalog includes data from Open Food Facts,
which is itself ODbL. Records keep the licence of their source, recorded in
the `sources` table: USDA FoodData Central and openFDA are public domain.
